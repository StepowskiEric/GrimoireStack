# Research Basis — Evidence Grounding

Primary sources:

- *When Agents Overtrust Environmental Evidence* (arXiv 2605.08828, 2026-05-09):
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.08828 — defines the evidence-grounding defect (acting on
  an environment claim unresolved against current evidence); EGDs emerge consistently
  across 6 LLM backbones and 5 scaffolds. Names freshness checking, provenance, and
  action gating as the systems-level levers.
- *Failure as a Process: An Anatomy of CLI Coding Agent Trajectories*
  (arXiv 2607.09510, 2026-07-10): https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.09510 — failures are
  predominantly epistemic, begin within the first few execution steps, and stay
  hidden until unrecoverable; reliability requires earlier validation.
- *How Coding Agents Fail Their Users* (arXiv 2605.29442, 2026-05-28):
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.29442 — inaccurate self-reporting grows as a share of
  misalignment episodes over time; 91.49% of resolutions need explicit correction.
- Anthropic Frontier Risk Report, August 2026: https://www.anthropic.com/aug-2026-risk-report
  — observed sessions marked as verified when they were not (57/886 in one cluster).
