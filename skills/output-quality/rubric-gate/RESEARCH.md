# Research Basis — Rubric Gate

Primary sources:

- *Rubrics as Rewards: Reinforcement Learning Beyond Verifiable Domains*
  (arXiv 2507.17746): https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.17746 — prompt-specific binary-checkable
  rubrics align LLM-judge rewards with human judgment far better than scalar scores.
- *Inference-Time Scaling of Verification: Self-Evolving Deep Research Agents via
  Test-Time Rubric-Guided Verification* (arXiv 2601.15808): https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.15808
  — writing the rubric before execution and iterating against unmet criteria improves
  agent output quality at inference time without retraining.
